# Historical Portfolio Data Dictionary

## Date

Observation date.

Frequency:
Daily

## Australian_Equity

Australian equity market return/index.

Portfolio weight:
30%

## International_Equity

International developed-market/global equity return/index.

Portfolio weight:
40%

## Bonds

Fixed-income market return/index.

Portfolio weight:
20%

## Cash

Australian cash/short-term interest return series.

Portfolio weight:
10%

## Data conventions

- Dates must be unique.
- Dates must be chronological.
- Missing observations must be investigated.
- Market holidays may result in missing observations.
- All series should use consistent observation dates.
- Price/index series are converted into periodic returns before portfolio risk calculations.
